Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BreakAlley is a break aggregator, not a data business. This page explains what we collect, why, and what we never do with it. Short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the site, use no third-party trackers, and never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account info — if you create an account, we store your email address and a securely hashed password. That's it.
  • Your activity — saved breaks, followed breakers, and searches, so the site can show you your stuff.
  • Push notification preferences — if you enable alerts, we store your push subscription so we can notify you when a followed breaker goes live.
  • First-party analytics — we log outbound clicks, page views, saves, follows, and searches in our own database to understand what's working. All of it stays on our servers.
  • Feedback and suggestions — anything you send through our forms, so we can read and act on it.

What we don't do

  • No third-party trackers. No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad-network beacons. Our analytics are first-party only.
  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • No selling on BreakAlley. We're a pure aggregator — breaks happen on YouTube, Twitch, eBay, and Fanatics Live. We don't process payments for breaks (Pro subscriptions are handled by our payment provider).

Cookies

We use a single signed session cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links to eBay may include an affiliate identifier, which means we may earn a commission if you buy through them — at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure for details.

Your control

  • You can change your email or password anytime on your account page.
  • You can delete your account anytime from the same page — this removes your profile, saves, follows, and notification settings.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above. For questions about your data, use the feedback form on any page.
