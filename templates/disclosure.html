Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 2026

📢 Some links on BreakAlley — particularly links to eBay listings — may earn us a commission if you make a purchase through them, at no extra cost to you.

How it works

BreakAlley is free to use, and keeping it that way costs money (servers, data, development). One way we keep the lights on is affiliate partnerships — currently the eBay Partner Network.

When you tap a break that lives on eBay, the outbound link may include our affiliate identifier. If you then buy something on eBay, eBay pays us a small commission out of their own cut. You pay exactly the same price either way — the commission comes from eBay, not from your pocket.

What this doesn't change

  • Rankings are never for sale. Affiliate status has zero influence on which breaks appear, their order, or our recommendations. The feed is driven by what's live and upcoming, period.
  • Most links aren't affiliate links. Breaks on YouTube, Twitch, and Fanatics Live link straight to the source with no affiliate identifier.
  • We're transparent about it. This page exists so you always know how the site makes money. If our affiliate relationships ever change, we'll update this page.

Pro subscriptions

BreakAlley also offers an optional Pro subscription with extra perks (unlimited follows, push alerts). That's a direct subscription — no affiliate involved.
