New to box breaks?

A box break is simple: a breaker opens sealed sports cards live on stream, and you buy a piece of the action — a team, a random slot, or the whole box. Whatever gets pulled for your spot is yours, shipped to you.

Formats

Pick Your Team (PYT)

You choose your team (or teams) and pay per team. Every card pulled for your teams is yours. Popular teams cost more — you're paying for the odds.

Random Team

You buy a slot at one flat price. Teams are randomized live before the break, so everyone pays the same and luck decides who you get.

Personal

The entire box is yours. Every card pulled goes to you. The priciest option, but nothing is shared.

Case Break

A full sealed case (multiple boxes) split across many slots. Bigger card pools, bigger hits, more spots to buy.

Box Break

The catch-all term: one or more boxes opened live, with cards distributed according to the format above.

Platforms

eBay

Buy your slot like any other listing — auction or Buy It Now. The break itself streams on the seller's channel, usually YouTube. Most structured way to buy in.

YouTube

Breakers run scheduled live shows. Buy slots through the links in the stream description or the breaker's site, then watch the break live.

Twitch

Live-first streaming. Buy through the breaker's site, then watch and chat in real time. The most interactive of the bunch.

Fanatics Live

Fanatics' own live-shopping platform. Buy your spot and watch the break in the same app.
